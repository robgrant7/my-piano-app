Welcome to Sunny Shore Piano

Sunny Shore Piano is a beach-themed learning synthesizer. Practice with the falling bubble guide on the screen positioned above your physical piano.

Keyboard Mapping (for computer keys):

White keys map to the home row starting at A, and black keys map to the letter/symbol rows above:

White keys: A S D F G H J K L ; ' Z X C V
Black keys: W E T Y U O P [ ] \

Acoustic Piano Learning:

  • Mic Listening: Enable the Mic in the header so the app listens to your actual piano.
  • Learn Mode: Pauses falling notes and waits for you to strike the correct key on your real piano before advancing!
  • Sound Presets: Play with tropical vibes: Steelpan, Marimba, Surf Synth, or Ocean Breeze.
  • Speed Control: Slow down the scrolling bubbles to learn at your own pace!

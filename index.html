Welcome to Aura Piano

Aura Piano is a high-fidelity web synthesizer. Play notes using your mouse/touchscreen or your computer keyboard.

Keyboard Mapping:

White keys map to the home row starting at A, and black keys map to the number/letter rows above:

White keys: A S D F G H J K L ; ' Z X C V
Black keys: W E T Y U O P [ ] \

Key Features:

  • Sound Presets: Toggle between acoustic, pads, retro lead synth, or FM piano.
  • ADSR Envelope: Tailor your sound curve (Attack, Decay, Sustain, Release).
  • Recorder: Record a session, clear it, or play it back.
  • Song Practice: Click a song in the list to follow along! Highlighted keys will show you which notes to press next.
